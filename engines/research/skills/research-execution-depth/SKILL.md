# Purpose
Scale the amount of Research work to the evidence claim and risk, not to verbosity or a ritual number of sources.

## Goal
Return a useful bounded evidence-backed result quickly when a small question is sufficient, while preserving the existing Research authority, provenance and verification laws.

## When to use
Select when an authorized Research assignment requests a short answer, normal synthesis or in-depth investigation. No new approval step is needed solely to choose depth.

## Inputs
Actual research question and accepted objective; current evidence/source access; intended claim; uncertainty/consequence; admissible destination capabilities; any explicit time/resource budget.

## Execution contract
- **Design mode:** choose depth from existing question, expected claim and known constraints; no new source, tool or artifact is required.
- **Execution mode:** only actual admitted machine-only source access and computation available to the assigned destination.
- **Evidence:** all discovered sources used for claims have provenance and citations; Research verification and explicit uncertainty remain mandatory under the existing workflow.
- **Unsupported mode:** if a promised evidence or runtime capability is unavailable, narrow the claim or return NOT_PROVEN/INSUFFICIENT; never invent results.

## Required outputs
One depth recommendation and the smallest Research deliverable sufficient to support the exact claim, with sources/evidence, limitation status, and next action if materially needed. Reuse existing Research release artifacts/contracts.

## Procedure
1. Choose `R1_QUICK` for one bounded question, minimal relevant open discovery, short cited findings and explicit unresolved facts. Avoid forced parallel agents, research matrices, multi-package phases or automatic extra rounds.
2. Choose `R2_STANDARD` by default for comparing meaningful independent evidence, recording contradictions, tracing claims to sources and producing the existing verified Research release.
3. Choose `R3_DEEP` for complex, consequential or conflicting questions needing several dependent questions, independent corroboration or reproducible experiments where actually applicable.
4. Regardless of depth, preserve the machine-only constitution, real source provenance, evidence-versus-finding distinction, limitation labeling, destination executability and independent verification requirements.
5. Stop when the claimed conclusion is appropriately supported or the evidence ceiling is reached; gaps do not automatically trigger repeat research.
6. Route to Canon only if the admitted downstream objective actually requires it. A standalone verified Research release is a valid finished outcome.

## Anti-patterns
Equating depth with word count; minimum source quotas; preselected source-site lists; compulsory expert interviews or human studies; multi-agent deployment for a simple question; infinite gap loops; claiming DEEP as a confidence guarantee.

## Verification
Was the chosen depth proportional to the actual question? Are significant claims tied to verified machine-accessed sources, unsupported claims and limits honest, and the result compatible with the existing Research authority and release workflow?
